If you get stuck

Raise your hand — helpers are walking the room. Show your screen rather than starting over; a stuck screen is the most useful thing you can show us.

What if I fall behind?

Nothing today is graded and nothing is sequential across students. If setup runs long, pair with a neighbour and watch — you'll still follow the exercise, and you can finish the install afterwards.

Every page remembers where you were, so you can come back to it after class.

Is anything I type here saved or sent anywhere?

No. These pages are plain files with no server and no analytics. Your progress ticks are stored in your own browser only, and clearing them is one click at the bottom of each guide.
